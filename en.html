Foxel Apps & Games

Calm, cozy Android apps and games.

Foxel Apps & Games makes small, calm and cozy apps and games for Android. There are three so far: Focus Room (a focus timer), Mırıl (a gentle quit smoking companion) and Bakedoku (a pastry-themed Sudoku). All three work without creating an account.

Our apps

Focus Room

Stay off your phone, focus, decorate your room.

A cozy focus timer for Android: put your phone face-down to earn double coins, then decorate your own little rooms. Works offline, no account, no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Foxel Apps & Games?

Foxel Apps & Games develops small, calm and cozy apps and games for Android. Contact: hardsteelgames@gmail.com.

Which apps are there?

Focus Room: a timer where you flip your phone to focus and decorate your room. Mırıl: a gentle cat and a daily notebook for people trying to quit smoking. Bakedoku: a calm Sudoku where every number is a pastry.

Are the apps free, and do I need an account?

All three are free to download and work without an account. Watching ads is optional, and some apps offer optional purchases. Details are on each app page.

Is there an iPhone version?

For now they are Android only; iOS versions are planned for later.

Where are the privacy policies?

Each app has its own privacy policy page; use the "Privacy policy" link at the bottom of each app page.
