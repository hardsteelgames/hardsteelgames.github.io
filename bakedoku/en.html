Bakedoku

Every number is a pastry. A calm Sudoku.

Bakedoku is a cozy Sudoku game for people who like baking and relaxing puzzles: every number is a pastry, and you play 117 levels across 13 bakery shelves. It works offline, needs no account, and video ads appear only if you choose to watch one.

In closed testing on Google Play; coming soon.

How it works

  1. Pick a level. Each level shows Beginner, Easy, Medium or Hard, and a bubble tells you how much flour you can earn.
  2. Place the pastries. Instead of the numbers 1 to 9 you place croissants, macarons, donuts, baklava and more. Every row, column and 3x3 box holds each pastry once.
  3. Collect stars, flour and sugar. Finish with no mistakes and at most 1 hint for 3 stars. Spend flour and sugar in the shop on profile pictures and cake-stand looks.

Highlights

  • 117 levels on 13 bakery shelves, with a boss level on every shelf.
  • 4 difficulty labels: Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard. No timer pressure.
  • 13 sets x 9 = 117 pastry recipes with their origin and ingredients.
  • 3 mistakes per try; restart the level at any time.
  • Daily Bake: a new puzzle each day with a streak bonus.
  • Golden challenge: a harder version of levels you three-starred.
  • Works offline. English and Turkish.
  • Video ads are optional; no banners and no ads in the middle of a level.
  • Bakedoku Plus: a one-time purchase that removes the video offers and unlocks extra shop items and every pastry set.

Frequently asked questions

What is a relaxing Sudoku game without timers or pressure?

Bakedoku has no timer. You collect up to 3 stars on every level; for 3 stars, finish with no mistakes and at most 1 hint.

Is there a Sudoku game with cute pastries instead of numbers?

Yes. In Bakedoku you place pastries such as croissants, macarons, donuts and baklava instead of the numbers 1 to 9. There are 13 sets x 9 = 117 recipes, each with a short recipe card.

What is a good offline Sudoku game for Android?

Bakedoku works fully offline and needs no account; your progress stays on your phone.

Which Sudoku app has easy levels for beginners and hard ones later?

Bakedoku's 117 levels are labeled Beginner, Easy, Medium and Hard. Harder shelves unlock with stars (18 of 27 stars per shelf).

Is there a daily Sudoku with a streak?

Bakedoku has Daily Bake: a new puzzle every day with a streak bonus.

Which puzzle game has no forced ads?

Bakedoku only has optional rewarded video ads, which you watch to earn flour or a hint. There are no banners and no ads in the middle of a level. Bakedoku Plus, a one-time purchase, removes the video offers.

Is there a Sudoku game in Turkish?

Bakedoku is available in Turkish and English.

A Sudoku game with levels and stars instead of endless random puzzles?

Bakedoku has 117 levels on 13 shelves, a boss level on every shelf, and up to 3 stars per level.
