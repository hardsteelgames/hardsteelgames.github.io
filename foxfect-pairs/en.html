Foxfect Pairs

Find every pair while a fox cheers you on. No timer in the levels.

Foxfect Pairs is a cozy memory card-matching game for Android players aged 13 and up, where you find every pair before your moves run out while Foxi the fox reacts to what happens, with no timer in the levels and no internet needed.

Coming soon to Google Play. It will be free for Android. A closed test is being prepared.

Screenshots

How to play

  1. Turn two cards. A pair stays open; two different cards turn back. Remember their places.
  2. Watch your moves. Each turn costs one move. Find all pairs before the moves run out and earn up to 3 stars.
  3. Open boxes, collect decks. Coins you earn by playing open boxes with new card backs and helpers.

Highlights

  • 60 levels in 3 worlds (Sky, Mint, Dusk) on boards from 3×4 to 5×6.
  • No timer in the levels: only your number of moves is limited.
  • 12 special cards and 3 helpers: Peek, Extra move, Shield.
  • Endless mode: catch the twins of cards falling in three lanes; opens after level 10.
  • 18 card decks to collect: 6 Common, 3 Rare, 2 Epic, 7 Legendary. One free box a day.
  • Foxi, a fox with headphones, reacts to matches, mistakes and every special card.
  • Plays offline without an account. No forced ads; boxes are never sold for money.
  • English and Turkish; reduce motion, sound and vibration settings. Android 7.0 and newer.

12 special cards

Mischievous (8)

Burned cookie, Spilled drink, Dusty shelf, Yarn, Worms, Mouse, Broom and Open window. They take a move, make a card sticky, mix cards up or hide pairs again.

Helpful (4)

Treat gives an extra move, Letter shows a pair, Candle lights up all cards for a moment and Sweater blocks the next bad card.
