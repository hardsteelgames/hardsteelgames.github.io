1. Hangi veriler işleniyor?

a) Yalnızca telefonunda tutulanlar. Seviye ilerlemen ve yıldızların, coin ve enerji miktarın, desteler, jokerler ve kutular, Sonsuz mod en iyi skorun ve ayarların (dil, müzik ve ses düzeyi, titreşim, hareketi azaltma). Bu veriler bize gönderilmez; onları göremeyiz.

b) Toplamadıklarımız. Hesap, ad ya da e-posta istemeyiz. Konum, kişiler, kamera, mikrofon, fotoğraf ya da dosyalarına erişmeyiz. Reklam kimliği, analiz ya da hata raporlama aracı kullanmayız.

c) Google Play ve Android. Oyunu Google Play'den indirdiğinde ya da güncellediğinde Google kendi politikasına göre veri işler. Android ayarlarında kullanım ve tanılama paylaşımını açtıysan, Google Play bize toplu çökme ve performans raporları gösterebilir; kapalı testte bize Google Play üzerinden yazdığın geri bildirimi de görürüz. Android yedeklemesi açıksa Android, oyunun kayıt dosyasını Google hesabındaki yedeğine kopyalayabilir; bu yedeği Google yönetir, biz erişemeyiz. Google Gizlilik Politikası.

d) Bize yazarsan. E-posta adresini ve mesajını yalnızca sana yanıt vermek ve sorunu çözmek için kullanırız; istediğinde sileriz.

Oyun verileri telefonunda, uygulamaya özel alanda durur ve oyun tarafından ağ üzerinden gönderilmez. Hiçbir yöntem %100 güvenli değildir.

Foxfect Pairs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · App: Foxfect Pairs (Android, com.foxel.foxfectpairs) · Developer: Foxel Apps & Games · Contact: hardsteelgames@gmail.com

In short: Foxfect Pairs needs no account and collects no data from you. Your progress stays on your phone. This version has no ads, purchases, analytics or tracking, and the game has no internet permission.

1. What data is processed?

a) Stored only on your phone. Your level progress and stars, coins and energy, decks, helpers and boxes, your best Endless score and your settings (language, music and sound volume, vibration, reduce motion). This data is not sent to us and we cannot see it.

b) What we do not collect. We do not ask for an account, name or email address. We do not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. We use no advertising ID, analytics or crash reporting tool.

c) Google Play and Android. When you download or update the game from Google Play, Google processes data under its own policy. If you turned on usage and diagnostics sharing in Android settings, Google Play may show us aggregated crash and performance reports; during a closed test we also see the feedback you send us through Google Play. If Android backup is on, Android may copy the game's save file to the backup of your Google account; Google manages that backup and we cannot access it. Google Privacy Policy.

d) If you write to us. We use your email address and message only to answer you and solve the problem, and delete them when you ask.

2. Permissions

The game asks you for no permission and does not connect to the internet.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; when you visit it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address under its privacy statement. We use no cookies, analytics or ads on the site.

4. Retention and deletion

Your game data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. You can delete an Android backup in the backup settings of your Google account. We hold no account or cloud data of yours.

5. Your rights

Under Turkish data-protection law (KVKK) and, if you are in the EU/UK, the GDPR, you have the right to know whether your data is processed, and to access, correct, delete and object to processing. Write to hardsteelgames@gmail.com; we reply within a reasonable time and at most 30 days.

6. Children

Foxfect Pairs is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you notice a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Game data stays on your phone in the app's private storage and the game does not send it over the network. No method is 100% secure.

8. Changes

If we add optional rewarded ads, in-app purchases, usage statistics or crash reporting later, we will update this policy before that version is released, change the “Last updated” date and ask for your consent where required.

9. Contact

Questions and requests: hardsteelgames@gmail.com
